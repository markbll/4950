# Package scope

All prices are in Australian dollars **per month, excluding GST**. Advertising spend (Google, Meta) is **always separate**, paid directly by the client to the platform, and agreed before any campaign goes live. Source of truth for website copy: `src/data/packages.ts`.

**No package promises rankings, Map Pack positions or a number of leads.** Reports state what was done and what changed, with sources.

| | Bronze — Get Found Locally | Silver — Own Your Area | Gold — Lead Your Region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | **$495/month + GST** | **$995/month + GST** | **$1,795/month + GST** |
| Best for | Sole traders / small teams, one main area | Established businesses, up to 3 service areas | Up to 3 locations or a wide service area |
| Includes | — | Everything in Bronze | Everything in Silver |

## Bronze — Get Found Locally ($495/month + GST)

- Google Business Profile optimisation (categories, services, service areas, hours, photos, Q&A)
- 4 Google Business Profile posts per month
- Local keyword map for 1 service area
- NAP audit and core citation fixes
- Website local SEO check
- Review-generation setup and review response drafting
- 1 local SEO article or service-area page update per month
- 2 social posts per month
- Search Console, Analytics and GBP insights monitoring
- Monthly local visibility summary; quarterly review

## Silver — Own Your Area ($995/month + GST)

Everything in Bronze, plus:

- Local keyword map for up to 3 service areas
- Local competitor research and local rank tracking
- Expanded citation building
- 2 local content pieces per month
- 8 social posts per month on Facebook and Instagram
- 1 email or SMS campaign per month
- Local Google Ads setup (ongoing management optional, quoted separately)
- Conversion tracking for calls, forms, bookings and directions
- Monthly strategy meeting and report; quarterly local campaign planning

## Gold — Lead Your Region ($1,795/month + GST)

Everything in Silver, plus:

- Up to 3 Google Business Profile locations or expanded service areas
- 4 local content or SEO assets per month
- 12 social posts per month, including LinkedIn where appropriate
- Local Google Ads management (including Local Services Ads where eligible)
- Local Meta Ads management
- Review program and reputation monitoring
- Local link and partnership building
- Up to 2 hours per month of minor website updates
- 2 email or SMS campaigns per month
- 1 local campaign per month
- Full monthly local dashboard; priority support

## Big Cat Growth — Custom

For 4+ locations, franchises, website rebuilds, eCommerce and larger ad budgets. Scoped and quoted individually.

## Out of scope unless quoted

Ad spend; photography/video shoots; logo/brand refresh (beyond consistent listing descriptions); website rebuilds and eCommerce; translation (must be done or checked by a qualified translator); community management (replying to comments/DMs); event logistics; printing.
